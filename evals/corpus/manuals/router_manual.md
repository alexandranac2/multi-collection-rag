# Northwind HomeLink Router — User Manual

## Unpacking

The box contains the router, a 12 V power adapter, one Ethernet cable and this manual.

## Installation

Connect the grey WAN port to your modem with the Ethernet cable, then plug in the
power adapter. The status light blinks white while starting and turns solid blue
when the router is online, usually within two minutes.

## Connecting to Wi-Fi

The default network name and password are printed on the sticker underneath the
router. The 5 GHz network is faster; the 2.4 GHz network reaches further through walls.

## Factory reset

To restore factory settings, hold the recessed reset button for 10 seconds with a
paperclip until the light flashes red. All custom settings, including the Wi-Fi
password, are erased.

## Firmware updates

Firmware updates install automatically between 02:00 and 04:00. You can also
update manually from the admin page under Settings → Firmware.

## Troubleshooting

A solid red light means no internet connection: check the modem and the WAN cable.
An orange light means the router is overheating; move it to a ventilated place.

## Warranty

The router has a two-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Water
damage and physical damage are not covered.
