# Master Services Agreement (fictional sample)

## 1. Term

This agreement starts on the effective date and runs for an initial term of
24 months. It renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either
party gives notice of non-renewal.

## 2. Termination

Either party may terminate for convenience with 90 days' written notice. Either
party may terminate immediately if the other party materially breaches the
agreement and fails to cure the breach within 30 days of written notice.

## 3. Fees and payment

Invoices are issued monthly in arrears and are payable within 45 days. Late
payments accrue interest at 1% per month.

## 4. Service levels

The provider guarantees 99.9% monthly availability. For each full 0.1% below
this target, the customer receives a service credit of 5% of the monthly fee,
capped at 50%.

## 5. Confidentiality

Confidential information may only be used to perform this agreement. The
obligation survives termination for five years.

## 6. Limitation of liability

Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months before
the claim. The cap does not apply to breaches of confidentiality or to gross negligence.

## 7. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Netherlands. Disputes are settled
by the courts of Amsterdam.
