# Northwind Employee Handbook

All company names and figures in this document are fictional.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Outside core hours, employees choose
when to work, provided they log 40 hours per week.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require written approval from a department head and are reviewed every six months.

## Vacation

Full-time employees receive 25 paid vacation days per calendar year. Up to five
unused days carry over into the next year and expire on March 31.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for
absences longer than three consecutive working days.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers receive 6 weeks. Leave can start up to four weeks before the due date.

## Expenses

Business travel is booked through the travel portal. Receipts must be submitted
within 30 days; reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary. Meals are
reimbursed up to 60 EUR per day while travelling.

## Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop, which is replaced every three years. Lost or
stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## Security

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are never shared. Multi-factor
authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN and the code repository.
Suspected phishing emails are forwarded to security@northwind.example.
